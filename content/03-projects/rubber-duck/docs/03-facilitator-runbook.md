# Facilitator runbook

## Pre-event
- Confirm 15+90 minute slot, team count, projector/input/audio, participants' Copilot access, agent sharing status and approved submission mechanism.
- Validate offline launcher and local synthetic files on corporate laptop on corporate network and disconnected state.
- Dry-run with someone who didn't build the tools; observe any confusion and actual mission times.
- Prepare at least one non-sensitive fictional case per team if no one wants to share real operational examples.
- Assign someone other than the presenter to monitor time/room/questions if possible.

## Opening line
'We are not here to see who can write the cleverest AI prompt. We are here to understand a problem better before deciding whether it deserves our money, time or attention.'

## Interventions
- Team demands an AI solution immediately → 'Which observation supports that this is the cause?'
- Team is stuck → provide two synthetic starting pains: repetitive customer contact or delayed handoffs.
- Copilot hallucinates → ask group to label unsupported propositions and treat as open questions.
- Team overuses model → stop interaction for 90 seconds and have each person state their own judgement.
- Debate gets personal → challenge the statement/evidence, not the person.
- No time for all pitches → 60-second pitches and collect passports for later discussion; do not silently extend event.

## Surprise Quack Attack cards
1. 'What if this delay is a control functioning as designed?'
2. 'What if the actual cost sits with a different team?'
3. 'What if 80% of the delay were waiting for missing information? (Hypothetical only.)'
4. 'Your proposed fix helps staff but makes life harder for customers. What changes?'
5. 'Imagine you cannot buy any software for 12 months. What would you do?'
6. 'A colleague shows evidence that contradicts your top assumption. Update your view.'

## Closing
'You do not need certainty today. You need a sharper hypothesis, evidence you still owe yourselves, and a cheap way to learn.'

## Post-event
Export aggregated/non-sensitive passports; identify experiment owners and review dates; follow up in 30 days via approved channel. Explicitly document what was validated versus merely proposed.

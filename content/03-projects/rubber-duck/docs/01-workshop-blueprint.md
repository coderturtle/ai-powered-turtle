# Workshop blueprint and educational design

**North star:** Don't ask AI for the answer. Use it to find a better question.
**Operating rhythm:** THINK (human) → CHALLENGE (AI) → DECIDE (human).
**Roles:** Duck Wrangler (operates team Copilot), Evidence Keeper (classifies claims), Chief Quacker (contrarian), Hatchery Lead (experiment/pitch); rotate between missions.
**Delivery:** 15-min live presentation + 90-min activities. Assume 4–6 teams of 3–5, but make team count configurable. Use 90-second pitches only if time allows.

| Event clock | Mission | Mode | Required outcome | Human gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 00–15 | Provocation | Plenary | Fictional process-archaeology demo; differentiate facts/inference | Facilitator reveals missing evidence |
| 15–25 | Silent Pond | Individual → team | Three friction observations/person; one selected team challenge | Team selects and writes why |
| 25–40 | Duck Detective | Team | Problem card: symptoms, stakeholders, facts, unknowns | Team approves/edits problem |
| 40–55 | Mirror | Team | As-is process map, exceptions, handoffs, two verification questions | Annotate supported vs inferred |
| 55–70 | Quackquisition | Individual → team | Independent sceptic/customer/control critiques; strongest counterargument | Team accepts/rejects/flags unknown |
| 70–90 | Hatchery | Team | Simplify/augment/reinvent options; cheapest meaningful experiment | Team chooses + documents rationale |
| 90–105 | Duck-Off | Plenary | 90-sec pitch, criteria-based ranking, owners and next steps | Leadership chooses hatch/incubate/duck |

## Facilitator timeboxing
- Announce every mission with goal, time left, input, required output, and fallback.
- In Silent Pond: *no Copilot until first independent notes written*.
- In Duck Detective: stop solution-jumping; ask 'what did we observe?'.
- In Mirror: distinguish documented flow from real observed work; arrows do not constitute facts.
- In Quackquisition: require individuals to critique separately for first ~5 mins; one-minute round-robin.
- In Hatchery: insist the **simplify without AI** option exists. Value is marked estimated or unknown; no invented precision.
- Duck-Off: score *problem importance*, *evidence strength*, *experiment learning value* separately, 1–5. Score is discussion aid, not investment decision.

## Presentation hook (15min)
0–2 paradox: building is cheaper, choosing the right problem remains hard.
2–4 rubber duck asks one sharper question than an agreeable assistant.
4–10 synthetic account-transfer case; reconstruct with Mirror; identify claims based on incomplete sources.
10–13 release hidden evidence to invalidate an easy diagnosis; show correction and a smaller experiment.
13–15 hand over mission: 'Don't arrive with a solution; arrive with a friction.'

## Deliverable: Opportunity Passport
Required: title, initial observation, selected problem, observed facts with source, assumptions, affected people, as-is flow, unknowns, counterargument, interventions, next experiment, owner and next review. Every field can be 'unknown' but not implicitly fabricated.

## Ethical/operational constraints
Participants can refuse to use sensitive examples, use synthetic cases, or omit details. No individual-level profiling or rating of people. Do not present AI outputs as validated business evidence. The project is not an assessment of staff performance.

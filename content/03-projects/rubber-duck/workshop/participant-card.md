# Operation Rubber Duck — Participant card

**Rule:** Independent thinking → AI challenge → team decision.

1. Silent Pond (individual): write three real frictions, with a concrete example; **no Copilot yet**.
2. Duck Detective (team): choose one problem, distinguish symptoms from diagnoses.
3. Mirror (team): map the actual process. Mark all inferred steps.
4. Quackquisition (individual then team): split sceptic/customer/control-owner perspectives; bring one hard challenge each.
5. Hatchery (team): simplify, augment, reinvent. Pick a cheap test, not a polished pitch.
6. Duck-Off (all): 90 seconds explaining changed understanding, strongest contrary argument and test.

**Avoid:** real PII, customer/account data, confidential case identifiers, unapproved uploads. Use generalized or synthetic descriptions as needed.
**If Copilot is inaccessible:** discuss using the printed challenge cards; progress should not stop.
**End state:** an Opportunity Passport reviewed by humans.

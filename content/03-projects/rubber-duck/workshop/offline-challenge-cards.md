# Offline cards (print 1 per team)

**Duck:** What did you personally observe? Who is affected? What evidence challenges the proposed cause? Can you describe the problem with no solution words?

**Mirror:** What starts work? Name the next five steps, handoffs, queues, exceptions, and which steps you cannot prove. What's different between policy and practice?

**Quackquisition — Sceptic:** What would disprove the team's explanation? What else could be causing the symptoms?

**Quackquisition — Customer:** Does this optimise the experience customers care about? Who suffers elsewhere if we change it?

**Quackquisition — Control owner:** Which steps protect the firm or customers? What risk increases if removed?

**Hatchmaster:** Remove step / improve existing step / redesign end to end. Which cheap experiment can distinguish these? What outcome would lead you to stop?

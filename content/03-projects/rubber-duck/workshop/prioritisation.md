# Duck-Off scoring (1–5, separately)

1. Problem importance: 1 little impact; 3 meaningful local friction; 5 material cross-team customer/operational/risk issue. Require rationale.
2. Evidence strength: 1 anecdote/unverified; 3 some supporting examples; 5 compelling relevant validated evidence. Missing data → low score, not zero enthusiasm.
3. Experiment learning value: 1 vague or huge commitment; 3 practical measurement; 5 cheap, safe, decision-changing hypothesis test.

**No AI-generated single score.** Humans record three numbers and reason. Final triage: HATCH (bounded experiment), INCUBATE (needs evidence), DUCK (do not pursue currently). Never claim scores imply funding approval.
